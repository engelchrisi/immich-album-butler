Restore

Restore only adds things: it creates missing albums and puts missing assets back. It never removes an asset or deletes an album.

1. Choose a backup

2. Choose what to restore

Off: only Immich albums are restored; your Butler album rules, groups and logins stay as they are now. On: they are replaced by the ones in this backup (config.toml); the current file is kept as config.toml.bak, so this can be undone.
